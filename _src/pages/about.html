About us

American know-how. Bosnian heart.

YU/GROW was founded by Edvin Dulas and Ajdin Mekic, two Penn State graduates and first-generation Bosnian Americans. Our parents fled a war-torn country so their children could have a better future. We built our careers in the United States, and now we're giving that knowledge back.

We run YU/GROW from the heart of Bosnia and Herzegovina, serving clients across the U.S., Europe and beyond. Every project helps a business grow, and helps create jobs for young Bosnians building careers in marketing and business.

Our mission

Help businesses everywhere grow with honest, data-driven marketing, and build the next generation of marketing talent in Bosnia and Herzegovina.

We grew up hearing what our parents gave up so we could have opportunities they never had. YU/GROW is how we honor that: by bringing the skills, tools and standards we learned in America to clients around the world, and by opening doors for young people at home who are ready to build a career.

  • Growth for our clientsMarketing methods proven in the U.S., powered by AI and tracked in your CRM, so you always know what your budget earns.
  • Honesty and transparencyClear reports, real numbers and a 3-month pilot before any long-term commitment. If something isn't working, we tell you and fix it.
  • Opportunity at homeWe hire and train young people in Bosnia and Herzegovina, giving them hands-on experience with international clients and modern tools.
  • Paying it forwardOur families got a second chance. We're passing it on, one client and one hire at a time.
