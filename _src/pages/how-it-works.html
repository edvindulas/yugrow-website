FAQ

Questions we hear often

What does "AI-powered" mean in practice?

We use AI tools to research audiences, write and test ad variations, score leads and analyze results. A person on our team reviews and approves everything before it goes live.

Do I need a CRM already?

No. If you have one, we connect to it. If you don't, we recommend one that fits your size and budget, set it up and train your team to use it.

How quickly will I see results?

Most clients see their first leads within 1–2 weeks of launch. Costs usually drop steadily over the 3-month pilot as campaigns are optimized.

Do you work with clients in my country?

Yes. We work with businesses across the US, the UK and Europe. Everything runs remotely, calls are scheduled in your time zone, and reports and dashboards are in English.

How does working together start?

Every partnership starts with a 3-month pilot. We launch your campaigns, set up tracking and show you real results. After the pilot, we sit down together and agree on next steps and a contract tailored to your timeline and goals.

How much ad budget do I need?

It depends on your goals and industry. We'll recommend a starting budget on your strategy call, and we can start small and scale up as results come in.
